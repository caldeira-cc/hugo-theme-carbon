Enterprise multi-cloud architectures require real-time visibility across heterogeneous environments while maintaining rigorous access controls and accessibility compliance.

## Project Scope & Objectives

The Multi-Cloud Governance Portal was conceived to unify telemetry and policy enforcement across hybrid cloud regions.

### Key Milestones Delivered:
1. **Unified Dashboard**: Single-pane management across AWS, Azure, and IBM Cloud.
2. **Accessible Data Grids**: High-density Carbon v11 DataTables supporting 50,000+ live resource nodes.
3. **Automated Remediation**: Instant policy enforcement triggers for security non-compliance.
